# Warm-Up 01 – Introduction

## What is DSA?

**DSA** stands for **Data Structures and Algorithms**.

* **Data Structure**: A way to organize and store data efficiently.
* **Algorithm**: A step-by-step procedure to solve a problem.

---

## Why Learn DSA?

* Improves problem-solving skills.
* Helps write efficient code.
* Frequently tested in software engineering interviews.
* Builds a strong programming foundation.

---

## Why is DSA Important?

* Optimizes time and memory usage.
* Helps solve complex problems efficiently.
* Required for technical interviews at many companies.
* Makes it easier to understand advanced computer science concepts.

---

## Learning Strategy

For each lesson:

1. Understand the concept.
2. Write notes.
3. Practice coding examples.
4. Solve problems.
5. Review regularly.

---

## Key Takeaways

* DSA is about solving problems efficiently.
* Consistency is more important than speed.
* Practice is essential for mastering DSA.
* Understanding concepts is better than memorizing solutions.
